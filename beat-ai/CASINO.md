# Beat AI: Casino

Beat AI: Casino is a social-casino expansion inside the existing Beat AI product.

## Current playable MVP

- Casino lobby with Blackjack live and Poker / Roulette / Beat the House surfaced as upcoming modes
- Playable blackjack engine
- 10,000 starting Royale Chips
- 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 chip bets
- Blackjack 3:2 payout
- Dealer stands on 17
- Local bankroll, wins, and casino-level progression
- AI dealer personality (Vincent)
- Mobile-first UI
- Explicit non-cash virtual currency model: chips cannot be redeemed, transferred, or cashed out

## Next build targets

1. AI Poker with opponent personalities and bluff profiles
2. Daily Beat the House bankroll challenge
3. Shared Beat AI XP/profile progression across daily game and casino
4. Casino career tiers and unlockable environments
5. Leaderboards and authenticated persistence
6. Cosmetic-only / subscription monetization surfaces after legal/product review
